Overview

A Polymer Battery (also called Lithium Polymer Battery) uses a polymer electrolyte instead of a liquid one. This enables thinner, lighter, and safer battery designs, widely adopted in consumer electronics, medical devices, and electric mobility.

Technical Terminology

  • Polymer Electrolyte – A gel or solid electrolyte that improves safety and flexibility.
  • Energy Density – The amount of energy stored per unit volume or mass.
  • Cycle Life – Number of complete charge/discharge cycles before capacity drops significantly.
  • PCM (Protection Circuit Module) – Prevents overcharge, over-discharge, and short circuit.
